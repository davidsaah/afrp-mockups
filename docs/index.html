AFRP Platform — Design Delivery

The standard delivery, three ways to read one design: click through it, read the member's day, or read the operations walkthrough. Every document inlines the same design system byte-for-byte.

Use it
Integrated prototype interactive 61 screens across four lenses — Member, Club, Program, Federation. Live demonstrations: bylaw rule toggles, weighted delegation, the electronic ballot with inclusion-only receipt, versioned dues schedules, the abstention flip. Works on a phone.
Read it
The member experience narrative 21 screens following Nadia Khoury of the Detroit Club through one day — 4 November 2026 — so the rules are shown interacting rather than described one at a time. The operations experience narrative 21 screens for staff and officers, grouped by lens: federation, finance, club, programme, magazine — one object model seen from four angles. The bylaw drafting workbench narrative How a bylaws revision is drafted as a versioned ruleset and run against real history before anyone votes on it — delegate divergence, motions re-tallied, who is affected.
