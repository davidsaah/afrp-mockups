The Programs — by section, each with a charter

v2.0 · 19 Aug 2026 · program facts, committees and convention policies pulled from afrp.org on this date

Every program, organized into six sections — Medical, Cultural, Leadership, Educational, Camp, Convention — and every one carrying the same charter: a defined mission (quoted from afrp.org), a committee drawn from the membership (the real standing committees from afrp.org/get-involved, joined via the Committee Interest Form), a budget (real figures where published, demo-labelled where not), a list of activities, and defined metrics of success. Host entities stay visible as chips — ARFHSN confirmed, ARFECF proposed, and the fourth partner, Ramallah Foundation Inc., New York, est. 1943.

Programs cross-reference through shared rails, and four of those rails are drawn below as pipelines — the youth pipeline alone carries one member from Camp Ramallah at 13 to RBPN in their career. People and money in demo flows remain fictional; the program facts are real and sourced on every frame.
